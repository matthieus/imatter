"Task-first AI" means agreeing with AI, and with yourself, on what you're doing before you ask for help. Think about the conversation you would have with a coach working with you on your marathon training. A coach who doesn't know the race date, your fitness level, or how your training is going can't help much. Even though AI is not a person, AI interactions have many of the same unwritten rules and pitfalls as working with a person. To avoid disappointment, you and AI both need a shared understanding of the goal, constraints, results, and what's been tried before.

In practice, task-first AI means a fusion of AI help with project management, each benefiting the other. Your AI has purpose, and task management becomes a natural part of the work itself.

Getting help from AI inside your task management means _you_ start working inside your task management context as well. Adding files, pictures, and links to help AI help you means you also have that working material inside your plan. The tool becomes your natural project workspace. And updating the plan based on findings becomes a simple AI request.

Tools like ChatGPT integrations via MCP, Obsidian and Notion with plugins can help implement that idea. Task Matter implements task-first AI as its foundation. And a foundation means you can build a lot of nice things on top of it.
