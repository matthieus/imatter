---
title: "Claude Code is a bit like a consultant, or a cat"
date: 2026-06-05 13:00:00 +0100
---
Like cats and consultants, AI always lands on its feet.

The main constraint cats, consultants and AI have in common is they have to be quick. They rely on signals they recognise to quickly make a decision, and act. Understanding the full context is never an option. There is a significant side-effect to that constraint, and it is how frequently mistakes are made. Falling back on your feet becomes a critical skill.

Claude Code, more than Codex, is particularly good at recovering. When you point out gaps between reality and plan, it will just move on, and address these gaps with another plan, again skilfully using words that will minimise the issue. Even if, really, you've been duped. And sometimes it is a major blunder. As in, your whole app is broken. It's like a cat that will break all your lovely cupboard deco, ruin your curtains, and fall back on its feet after being shouted at. And then of course you have to feed it.

Anyways, it's obviously not a fair comparison. I eventually got my cat to re-arrange all my deco and replace my curtains. It even re-built a new house, with extension, and 4 bathrooms and no kitchen. While accidentally setting my car on fire.
